## 2. Market Analysis

### 2.1 Web3 Gaming Market Overview

The blockchain gaming market is rapidly growing by combining token-based economies with the concept of digital asset ownership. In particular, **HTML5-based casual games** can be executed instantly in mobile webviews without requiring installation, making them the **lowest-barrier entry point** within the Web3 gaming category on mobile.

Existing Web3 games operate as siloed dApps, forcing users to repeat wallet connection, token swaps, and onboarding for each title. TOTT solves this friction through a **unified hub and single-token economy**.

### 2.2 DeFi Market Statistics

The DeFi sector is experiencing exponential growth, particularly in the payments and infrastructure segments.

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Market Size (2023) | $14.35 Billion |
| Projected Market Size (2032) | Over $450 Billion |
| CAGR | Over 47.8% |
| Blockchain Technology Segment (2032) | Over $200 Billion |
| North American Market Share (2023) | Over 35% |
| High-Growth Trigger Segment | Payments (Projected CAGR: 50%) |

The 50% CAGR in the payments segment forms the strategic basis for TomaTok to **secure the gateway to DeFi adoption through a high-frequency-transaction game hub**.

### 2.3 Limitations of the Messenger Market

While WhatsApp dominates the global messenger market with 1.22B DAU and 1.35B MAU, regional messengers such as KakaoTalk and LINE remain trapped in the **"Frog in a Well"** phenomenon. Despite high domestic saturation, they fail to achieve global liquidity or cross-border integration due to linguistic and technical silos.

TomaTok addresses this constraint by leveraging an AI-powered real-time translation messenger supporting 120 languages as a **dual-purpose channel for global user acquisition (UA) and community formation around the game hub**.

## 5. Project Strategy

### 5.1 Technology Stack

Built on Solana, the platform secures the **high performance, low latency, and scalability** required to simultaneously support a global social network and a DeFi layer. It is particularly well-suited to the high-frequency transactions characteristic of gaming.

### 5.2 Gamification

TomaTok applies gamification mechanisms across the entire platform to convert passive content consumption into **active, engagement-based behavior**.

- **Points and Level System**: User activities such as gameplay, daily check-ins, friend invitations, and ad views are converted into points, and accumulated points raise the user's level
- **Daily and Weekly Missions**: A mission structure that encourages repeated visits and exploration of diverse games
- **Achievements and Badges**: TOTT-based rewards for completing specific game categories or reaching milestones, encouraging long-term engagement

By gamifying not only the games themselves but **the act of using the platform as a whole**, TomaTok creates a retention structure that does not depend on any single game.

### 5.3 GameFi Integration Model

GameFi is a concept that combines Game and Decentralized Finance (DeFi), referring to a structure in which in-game economic activities are directly connected to on-chain assets. TomaTok implements its GameFi model along three primary axes.

| Axis | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Tokenization** | In-game outcomes (scores, rewards, items) can be converted into TOTT or NFTs |
| **Liquidity** | Users can freely use their assets across external exchanges, DEXs, and DeFi protocols |
| **Participatory Governance** | TOTT holders take part in core decisions such as game listing policies, exposure weights, and settlement ratios |

Unlike conventional GameFi projects whose token economies were tied to a single game, TomaTok smooths out the volatility of individual game cycles through a **hub model in which multiple games share a single token (TOTT) and a single user pool**.

### 5.4 TOTT Utilization Scenarios

TOTT functions as the base currency for all value transfer within the TomaTok ecosystem. Major utilization scenarios include the following.

**User Side**

- Payments for in-game items and resources
- Receipt of rewards from in-game activities
- Unlocking premium content and features
- P2P transfers and gifting within the messenger
- Conversion to fiat or other assets through external exchanges

**Developer Side**

- Payment of game registration fees
- Receipt of in-game revenue settlements (weekly or monthly)
- Bidding for promotion slots
- Settlement of advertising exposure

**Platform Side**

- Exercise of governance voting rights
- Payment of transaction fees
- On-chain participation through staking

This multilayered utilization structure means that TOTT functions not as a single-purpose token but as **a payment, reward, and governance medium across the entire ecosystem**.

### 5.5 P2E (Play-to-Earn)

P2E is a model in which users acquire tokens or tokenizable assets through gameplay. Among games registered on the TomaTok game hub, those that adopt P2E follow these principles.

- **Activity-Based Rewards**: Rewards are issued based on the user's actual play activity (scores, mission completion, ranking achievements)
- **Issuance Cap Controls**: The daily and weekly reward issuance for each game is set following review by the BlockStars Foundation review committee, controlling inflation
- **Anti-Abuse Measures**: Bots, multi-accounts, and abnormal patterns are blocked through on-chain and off-chain monitoring
- **Optional Adoption**: Not every game is required to adopt P2E; non-P2E titles coexist within the same hub

While P2E transforms gameplay time into a value-generating activity, the **sustainability of rewards is determined by complex factors including game popularity, token market conditions, and issuance policy**, and no return is guaranteed.

### 5.6 T2E (Tap-to-Earn)

T2E is a model in which TomaTok-operated **tap-based casual games allow users to earn points through gameplay, with accumulated points convertible into TOTT**.

| Step | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Participate** | Users access TomaTok's built-in T2E games (tap games, mini challenges) |
| **2. Earn Points** | Platform points are accumulated through gameplay |
| **3. Convert to TOTT** | Once accumulated points reach a threshold, they can be converted into TOTT |
| **4. Utilize** | Converted TOTT can be used for in-game payments, transfers, or external exchange withdrawals |

T2E features include the following.

- **Low Entry Barrier**: Anyone can participate immediately without prior learning or capital investment
- **Ad Integration**: Some T2E games offer additional points through ad views, with advertising revenue forming part of the reward pool
- **Anti-Abuse Controls**: Hourly earning limits, device verification, and behavioral pattern analysis prevent automated abuse
- **Onboarding Tool**: T2E functions as a **gateway game** through which users unfamiliar with Web3 can naturally adopt TOTT and wallet usage

The point-to-TOTT conversion ratio, daily earning limits, and withdrawal conditions for T2E may be adjusted based on platform operational data, with prior notice provided in the event of any change.

### 5.7 User-Engagement-Centric Economy

TomaTok pursues a sustainable **user-engagement-centric economy** rather than a simple incentive model.

- **Self-Management (自己 管理)**: Empowering users to directly manage their own consumption and saving habits
- **Self-Centered Utility**: Providing personalized services and utilities based on user platform engagement data, including play, learning, and community activities

The P2E model in 5.5 and the T2E model in 5.6 are concrete implementation models of this user-engagement-centric economy, providing a structure in which users directly control their own activity data and recover its value. **The game hub functions as the core execution layer of the user-engagement-centric economy.**

### 5.8 Platform Triggers

TomaTok serves as a "fuse" for DeFi adoption through the following pathways.

- **Global Market Capture**: User acquisition through the high-frequency transactions of the game hub
- **Token Governance**: Establishing TOTT as the base utility of the ecosystem
- **Asset Management Diversity**: Providing a variety of decentralized financial instruments

## 7. Smart Contract and On-Chain Staking Protocol

The TOTT staking protocol is implemented as an Anchor program on Solana. User staking positions, processing states, and account information are recorded in on-chain PDA (Program Derived Address) accounts.

This section is intended to describe on-chain identifiers and the technical structure. It does not guarantee token value, economic returns, payout sustainability, or any specific outcome.

### 7.1 On-Chain Identifiers

| Item | Address / Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Staking Program ID** | `8UzWbsBj6EgZVmFaFFqHowqD4QzRYH5UaZWPvAuVV6X` |
| **Pool Initialization Authority** | `85Etjjx3kj8fyJUdqe3Bz4rN2fDjA7L9sWndtumpAPUa` |
| **StakingPool PDA** | `BXgqKF9Vgfcej3jMLvBgy66E4JUXcLehA8QJ4KFimGKD` |
| **TokenVault PDA** | `CKPToXHH1PEbH9Wuwra9cBwheqST7UKBZL5WteZy5Uph` |
| **Token Program** | `TokenkegQfeZyiNwAJbNbGKPFXCWuBvf9Ss623VQ5DA` |

Users can verify on-chain state directly on Solana Explorer by looking up the Token Mint, Staking Program ID, StakingPool PDA, and TokenVault PDA.

### 7.2 Account Structure and PDA Model

Solana programs separate execution logic from state-storing accounts. The TOTT staking protocol uses the following PDA account structure.

| Account | PDA Seed | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **StakingPool** | `["staking_pool", token_mint]` | Records pool-wide state, total staked principal, and the token vault address |
| **TokenVault** | `["token_vault", token_mint]` | A program-controlled token account that holds the staked TOTT |
| **UserStake** | `["user_stake_v4", user, staking_pool, index]` | Records the individual staking position of each user |

By using the `index` value, a single user can simultaneously hold multiple independent staking positions.

### 7.3 Staking Positions and Time Convention

The protocol uses a 30/360 time convention for staking-period calculations: one month is treated as 30 days and one year as 360 days.
Supported staking durations are 6 months, 9 months, and 12 months. Each position is managed via a separate `UserStake` account and stores state values such as the start time, lock period, and last processing time on-chain.

### 7.4 Major On-Chain Actions

| Action | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Pool Initialization** | The administrator wallet creates the StakingPool and TokenVault PDAs |
| **Stake** | Transfers user TOTT to the TokenVault and creates a UserStake account |
| **Position Processing** | Processes the position state per on-chain rules after the relevant period has elapsed |
| **Unstake** | Handles maturity or early-unstake processing |
| **Account Cleanup** | After unstaking is complete, closes the PDA account and reclaims rent |

Failed on-chain transactions are not recorded as valid staking entries in the platform database.

### 7.5 Safeguards and Risk Management

| Safeguard | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Vault Balance Verification** | Verifies that the TokenVault balance is sufficient to cover the required processing amount |
| **Overflow Protection** | Uses checked arithmetic and intermediate u128 calculations for key math operations |
| **Ownership Verification** | The `has_one = user` constraint prevents tampering with another user's UserStake |
| **Time Verification** | Blocks anomalies in which the current time is earlier than the staking start time or the last claim time |
| **Token Account Verification** | Verifies the mint, owner, and frozen state of relevant token accounts |

These safeguards are technical measures that strengthen the integrity of protocol rules. However, general Web3 risks related to smart contracts, wallets, networks, market prices, liquidity, and regulatory changes remain.

## 10. Game Exposure and Ranking

Registered games are displayed across various exposure slots within the TomaTok app.

### 10.1 Exposure Slot Structure

| Slot | Position | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Main Banner** | Home top | Recommended, new, and event games (5–10 rolling) |
| **Category Rankings** | Home top tab | Top 10 by genre, language, and region |
| **New** | Home middle | Games registered within the past 30 days |
| **Trending** | Home middle | Games with sharp activity spikes within 24–72 hours |
| **Promotion Slot** | Ad area | Exposure based on developer TOTT bidding |

### 10.2 Exposure Policy

- **Fairness**: Clear visual distinction between non-advertised and advertised slots within the same category
- **Quality Standards**: Exposure restrictions for games with ratings below 2.5 or detected abnormal behavior (abuse)
- **Diversity**: A single developer may not occupy more than 30% of the main banner
- **Multilingual Priority**: Games matching the user's language settings are prioritized for exposure
- **Governance**: Changes to the exposure policy are decided through TOTT holder governance procedures
